## About

I'm a biomedical scientist with a background in Biology, Bioinformatics, and Biostatistics, and over three years of experience in clinical and translational research.

Throughout my career, I've collaborated with clinicians, epidemiologists, and bioinformaticians on multidisciplinary research projects involving clinical, epidemiological, and multi-omic data. As a first author, co-author, and peer reviewer of biomedical research, I've developed strong expertise in scientific literature appraisal, evidence interpretation, and scientific communication.

This GitHub profile showcases practical medical writing projects developed from real biomedical literature, reflecting my approach to communicating complex scientific evidence with clarity, accuracy, and scientific rigor.
