## About

I'm a biomedical scientist with a background in **Biology, Bioinformatics, and Biostatistics**, and over three years of experience in clinical and translational research.

My work has involved collaborating with clinicians, epidemiologists, and bioinformaticians on multidisciplinary research projects using clinical, epidemiological, and multi-omics data to generate and communicate biomedical evidence.

As a first author, co-author, and peer reviewer of scientific publications, I have developed strong expertise in scientific literature appraisal, critical interpretation of research findings, and evidence-based scientific communication.

My professional interests lie at the intersection of **clinical research, biomedical data interpretation, and medical writing**, combining scientific rigor with clear, accurate, and audience-focused communication.
